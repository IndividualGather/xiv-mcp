A job is a queue of tool calls that XIV MCP runs in the game, one after another, for as long as they take. It does not depend on the chat or the connection: you can close your AI app and the job keeps going.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `start_job` | Starts a job with its steps (tool and arguments). Later steps can use earlier results. |
| `list_jobs` / `get_job` | Jobs with state, progress and the current step; one job's steps, results, errors and log |
| `update_job` | For a pending or paused job: retries or skips the current step, adds steps or replaces the remaining ones |
| `pause_job` / `resume_job` / `cancel_job` | Controls a job |
| `wait` | A step that waits a number of seconds, or until a time |

| | Step by step | As a job |
|---|---|---|
| **Tokens** | Every call and every result costs tokens. Waiting costs the most: to know whether something has happened, the assistant keeps asking. | Starting costs one call. The steps cost nothing. |
| **Surprises** | Handled as they come: full bags, a missing NPC, a price that moved. The assistant sees every result and adapts. | The job follows its plan. Something unexpected stops the step, and the job waits as *pending* until your assistant fixes it. |
| **Long tasks** | Each call has to finish within your app's time limit for one call, often a minute. | Steps can take hours. |
| **The chat** | Has to stay open. | Can be closed. |

Your settings in `/xivmcp` apply the same way to both.

**What to ask for:**

- **For long or repetitive work,** say "run it as a job": farming a dungeon, gathering and crafting a list, a route with several stops. It is far cheaper, and keeps going without the chat.
- **For work that needs judgement,** let the assistant go step by step, or ask it to check with you: selling at good prices, sorting out a full inventory, anything where the next step depends on what just happened.
- **Mixing both works well.** The assistant can look around step by step, start a job for the long stretch, and decide what to do with the result.

## In the game

`/xivmcp` → **Jobs** lists every job with its progress, and has **Pause**, **Resume** and **Cancel** buttons. Your standing spending approvals are listed there too.

## How jobs behave

- **Each step needs its own permission.** A step set to *Ask* waits for you. A step whose module is denied fails.
- **One acting step at a time.** While a step runs, other tool calls that change something are refused, so they cannot collide with the job. Tools that only read are not blocked by a running step, and neither are waiting steps such as routes. They still follow their own settings.
- **Failures wait for a fix.** When a step fails or is stopped, XIV MCP first gets your character out of any conversation or window the step left open (see `recover_game_state`), then the job becomes **pending** and waits for your assistant to retry, skip or change it.
- **Jobs survive reloads.** After a plugin reload, jobs come back paused.

## Passing results along

A string argument `"{{stepId.path}}"` takes the value from an earlier step's result, keeping its type. Inside a longer string, as in `"Made {{craft.crafted}} pies"`, it is replaced by the value as text.

Example: `prepare_craft_plan` (step id `plan`) → `gather_until` for the missing materials → `run_crafting_list` with `"list": "{{plan.list.id}}"`.
