## Buying

`buy_item` picks the vendor unless you name one: gil vendors first, then those in a city (quick to reach by aetheryte), then those in the zone you are in.

- **Gil shops** buy directly, within the gil limit you can set for **Market & purchases**.
- **Any other currency** (scrips, tomestones, seals) shows an approval window in the game while the shop is open, unless you gave a standing approval. See [Approvals](/docs/user/permissions/approvals).
- **As many as you can afford**: with a standing approval, `up_to` buys as many as the currency in hand pays for (at most the quantity asked) and finishes without an error, e.g. after farming tomestones whose yield varies.
- **Grand Company seals**: only your own company's Quartermaster is used. `buy_item` clicks through the seal exchange's rank and category tabs (Materials first) until the item is listed, and stops when your rank is too low for it. Items that open a separate quantity window, such as Ventures, aren't supported yet.
- **Vendors with several menus**, such as the tool merchants, ask for a topic first ("Purchase Disciple of the Hand Tools") and then for the shop ("Purchase Tools (Lv. 1-9)"). `buy_item` finds both from the shops that sell the item. If it can't tell, it lists the entries; `menu_option` then takes one entry or the whole path, such as `Purchase Disciple of the Hand Tools > Purchase Tools (Lv. 1-9)`.
- **Gear** doesn't stack, so it is bought one piece at a time. Pieces that go into the armoury chest count as bought.
- **Out of the line of sight**: when the vendor doesn't answer (a counter in the way), `buy_item` talks again without the game's line-of-sight check, then from right next to the vendor, as `interact_with_object` does.
- **Exchanges that need a quest**, such as the master recipe books, only appear in the shop once you have done that quest. If you haven't, `buy_item` stops before you travel and names the quest.

## Selling and undercutting

Undercuts follow **Penny Pincher**'s settings when it is installed (amount, rounding, minimum, HQ, never your own retainers), and are 1 gil otherwise. A price below half the recent Universalis average is refused, unless you allow it. You can also give a minimum price per item: an undercut below it is skipped, and it keeps prices safe while Universalis doesn't answer (then nothing is listed without a minimum, a price or your permission).

### Selling to a merchant

`sell_to_vendor` sells to an NPC merchant, as the inventory's *Sell* does while a shop is open. Open a merchant's shop first. It sells whole stacks, the smallest first, so the big stacks are what you keep. Merchants pay little, often 1 to 10 gil each, so check the market board first. Items merchants do not buy are left alone, and what you sold stays in the shop's buyback tab until you close the shop.
