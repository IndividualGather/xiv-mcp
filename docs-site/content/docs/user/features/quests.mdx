With **Questionable** installed, your assistant can do quests for you: one quest with every quest it needs first, the day's quests for an allied society (beast tribe) to raise your reputation with them, or the main scenario from where you are.

These tools have their own module, **Questionable**, under **Integrations maintained by XIV MCP**. Everything that changes something is denied until you allow it. They are only offered while Questionable is loaded.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `get_questing_status` | Whether Questionable is running, the task it is on (and what comes next), the quest it is on, its priority list, your next main scenario quest, the allied society allowances left today and your reputation with each allied society |
| `complete_quest` | Does a quest, with every quest it needs first, as a background job |
| `do_tribe_dailies` | Does the day's quests for one allied society as a background job, rank-up quests included |
| `start_main_scenario` | Starts on the main scenario and carries on until stopped |
| `stop_questing` | Stops Questionable once a fight is over |

Things to ask:

> "Do the quests I need to unlock Eureka."

> "Do today's Pelupelu quests."

> "Carry on with the main scenario. Stop when I say so."

## A quest and the quests before it

`complete_quest` works out the chain from the game's quest data: the quests the quest needs, the quests those need, and so on. It leaves out what you completed and starts a [background job](/docs/user/features/jobs) that does the rest in order.

Before it starts, it tells you:

- **The quests** it will do, in order.
- **Quests Questionable has no path for.** It does not start then: do those yourself first.
- **Duties** the quests need done first, such as dungeons and trials.
- **Levels:** a quest that needs a higher level than your current job has.
- **Quests you can no longer do**, because you completed another one that shuts it out, such as another Grand Company's quest.

When several quests share a name, such as the first quests of each starting city, it picks the one your character can do.

A quest counts as done only once the game itself says it is turned in: out of your journal and completed (a daily: completed today), on two checks in a row. `check_quests` reads the same live quest state, so right after a turn-in both agree.

### When Questionable needs you

Some steps Questionable leaves to you: a duty it cannot run (or cannot have [AutoDuty](/docs/user/features/dungeons) run, for example below the duty's item level), a solo duty, or something to do by hand. Questionable says so in chat. The job then stops and tells you what Questionable said. Do that step, then start again: the quests you completed are left out.

XIV MCP moves on from these steps by itself, as if you pressed Questionable's Skip (they are [workarounds](#workarounds)):

- **"Meld materia on …"** when that piece of gear (in your bags, armoury or worn) already carries materia.
- **Buying an ingredient** at a shop when your bags already held enough of it before the step (Questionable can otherwise hang in the open shop).
- **Crafting a turn-in** that was already in your bags in the asked quality before the step. Items Questionable crafts or buys for the step itself are left to it: it finishes such a step on its own. Questionable has Artisan craft every item of the quest otherwise, again, which uses up materials meant for other turn-ins. For the same reason, when Questionable crafts the items itself with Artisan's list for the quest, XIV MCP stops the list once the step's items are in your bags.

When Questionable stops making progress, for example after a teleport took you to the wrong place, XIV MCP starts it again: when you stand in the same spot for two minutes without talking, fighting, gathering, crafting, a cutscene or a zone change. When Questionable stops because vnavmesh found no path (often while still flying), XIV MCP waits until you have landed and starts it again. Questionable also often stops by itself after a step it hands off, such as a craft, a cutscene or a dialogue choice, although the quest goes on. When it has stood still for 10 seconds after such a step, XIV MCP starts it again. After three restarts in a row the job stops.

A quest can only be turned in on the job it was accepted on. A class quest's craft step can leave you on another crafter, so before Questionable goes on with an accepted quest, XIV MCP switches back to that job. When you hand in a high-quality item, the game asks "Do you really want to trade a high-quality item?"; during a quest conversation XIV MCP answers Yes. Both are workarounds too.

## Allied society quests

`do_tribe_dailies` does the day's quests for one allied society, to raise your reputation with them:

<Steps>
<Step>

**Quests you already accepted** for them.

</Step>
<Step>

**Rank-up and story quests** that open once the reputation bar is full (shown in blue in the game). They come before the dailies, because a full bar gains nothing more until the rank goes up. They use no allowance.

</Step>
<Step>

**The day's daily quests**, as many as your allowances left allow. Each issuer offers a few quests a day. Questionable knows which ones, and XIV MCP does those.

</Step>
</Steps>

After each quest it looks again, so a rank-up reached on the way is done too, and so are the new rank's dailies. Allowances come back at the daily reset, at 15:00 UTC.

If you have not unlocked an allied society yet, it names the quest that unlocks them. `complete_quest` can do that quest, with the quests before it.

## The main scenario

`start_main_scenario` starts Questionable on your next main scenario quest. It carries on with the next ones, and with the quests on its own priority list first, until you stop it or it cannot go on, at a duty or a level it does not have. To stop at a certain quest, use `complete_quest` with that quest instead.

## Jobs

A quest remembers the job you accepted it on, and Questionable picks that job without looking at levels. So before a quest is accepted, XIV MCP switches to a gearset of a job that may do it: your current job when it may, else the highest level one. Gathering quests are always done as Miner or Botanist, never as Fisher, because Questionable gathers them on land. A gathering quest you already accepted as Fisher cannot be finished this way: abandon it, then start again. Your assistant can abandon it for you with `abandon_quest`, a tool of **Game & navigation**. Its progress is lost, and a daily quest's allowance is not given back.

When the quests need different jobs, XIV MCP does the quests of one job, then switches, after any fight is over.

## Workarounds

Most of what this page describes is XIV MCP planning the quests and watching Questionable. Some of it is XIV MCP stepping in where Questionable falls short: picking the jobs, accepting several ready quests at once, the steps it skips, the Yes to a high-quality turn-in, and stopping it after the last quest.

The job's result names the Questionable version it ran with and the workarounds it used, with how often (`workarounds`). Every workaround, with what Questionable does on its own and the Questionable version it was last checked against, is on [Plugins XIV MCP uses](/docs/user/plugins#questionable).

## Stopping

`stop_questing` stops Questionable, but never mid-fight: it lets the fight end first, then stops. A running quest job is paused, and `resume_job` carries on. Cancelling the job with `cancel_job` stops Questionable the same way.

## Questionable's priority list

XIV MCP gives Questionable the quests to do as its priority list. Your own list is put back when the job ends, however it ends.

Once the quests on its list are done, Questionable would go on to a quest of its own choosing, from your journal or the main scenario, and often teleports for it within a second or two. So on the last quest XIV MCP gave it, XIV MCP turns on Questionable's "stop after this quest" (the flag button in its quest window), and Questionable stops as that quest is done. Its window shows it stopping during that quest, and it says in chat when it stops; if you turn the flag off, XIV MCP turns it on again. When the job ends, the flag is off again.

<Callout type="warn">Questionable walks, teleports (which costs gil), talks to NPCs and fights on its own. Set up its combat module in Questionable's settings, and stay near the game while it quests.</Callout>
