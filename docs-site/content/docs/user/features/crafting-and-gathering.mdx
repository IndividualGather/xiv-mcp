Planning works on its own. Crafting and gathering go through **Artisan** and **GatherBuddy Reborn**, so their tools are only offered while those plugins are loaded, with their own modules under **Integrations maintained by XIV MCP**.

| Tool | What it does | Module |
|---|---|---|
| `plan_craft` | Plans a project: recipe tree, crafting order, stock from bags and retainers, where to get missing materials, bag space and batches, and the stats Artisan will craft with | Game data |
| `get_crafting_lists` / `set_crafting_list` / `delete_crafting_list` | Artisan's lists and state; creates, edits or deletes lists | Artisan |
| `craft_item` / `crafting_control` | Crafts an item a number of times; starts, pauses, resumes or stops a list | Artisan |
| `prepare_craft_plan` | Creates the Artisan list in the right order and works out each recipe's steps ahead of time with **Raphael** (the crafting solver built into Artisan), with the exact stats Artisan will use | Artisan |
| `run_crafting_list` | Runs an Artisan list to the end and reports what was crafted per recipe. Stops and fails when Artisan gets stuck | Artisan |
| `get_gather_lists` / `set_gather_list` / `delete_gather_list` / `set_auto_gather` | GatherBuddy Reborn's auto-gather lists, and auto-gather on or off | GatherBuddy Reborn |
| `gather_until` | Gathers until your bags hold the target quantities. Your other lists are paused and restored afterwards. It can also stop on something else in your bags, such as the aethersand that GatherBuddy reduces collectables into. If GatherBuddy turns itself off between timed node windows, it is started again. If GatherBuddy hangs, so your character stands idle for 6 minutes (`stall_minutes`) while no count changes, the step fails and names the items still missing and what GatherBuddy last said. Skip those items or get them another way. Waiting for a timed node does not count as hanging. A session length ends the step without failing, so a job can take breaks between `gather_until` steps. Fallback items, such as a fish, are only gathered while nothing else on the list can be, for example between timed node windows. | GatherBuddy Reborn |

## Filling the bags

With `"quantity": "fill"`, `plan_craft` plans as many as fit in your bags. For collectables, it also plans no more than can be turned in before the scrip cap, with the scrips per collectability tier.

## Is your gear good enough?

`prepare_craft_plan` plays each prepared solution through Artisan's simulator with the stats Artisan will craft with, assuming normal conditions, and shows what it reaches (for collectables, the tier and collectability). If a solution doesn't finish the craft, or a collectable stays below the lowest tier, it refuses before anything is crafted: better gear, food or a potion is needed first. `plan_craft` shows the same for solutions already prepared.

## Where materials come from

`plan_craft` lists gil vendors only when a gil shop sells the material. Vendors that take other currencies, such as gemstones or scrips, are listed as exchanges. Some of them only trade once you have unlocked them. Tradeable materials also list the market board. When a vendor won't trade, `buy_item` passes on what the game says: its system and error messages, not combat lines or chat that arrive at the same time.

## Equipment as an ingredient

Some recipes use a piece of equipment as an ingredient, for example Wrapped Crowsbeak Hammer (a Steel Crowsbeak Hammer) or Engraved Hard Leather Grimoire (a Hard Leather Grimoire). Artisan can't select equipment in the crafting log: the ingredient stays "Unselected" and the synthesis never starts. So:

- `plan_craft` marks these steps with `craftByHand`, and `prepare_craft_plan` warns about them and marks them in the list. Craft them yourself: open the recipe, select the ingredient and start the synthesis.
- `craft_item` refuses such recipes instead of hanging.
- `run_crafting_list` notices when Artisan stays busy but nothing gets crafted (no bag counts change) for `stall_minutes` (default 5). It stops Artisan and fails with the recipe it is stuck on and the likely cause. A paused list or a solution still being worked out doesn't count as stuck.

## A whole project as one job

> "Craft as many of this collectable as I can turn in before the scrip cap, gather what is missing, and turn them in."

Your assistant can run this as one [background job](/docs/user/features/jobs):

1. `prepare_craft_plan` with `"quantity": "fill"` for a collectable,
2. `gather_until` or `transfer_retainer_items` for the materials,
3. `run_crafting_list`,
4. `turn_in_collectables` (stops before the scrip cap),
5. `buy_item` with a standing approval, and
6. `sell_item`.

## Pausing and resuming a crafting step

Artisan always starts a list from the top. So when a job's `run_crafting_list` step runs again, after a pause or a retry, it continues instead: it remembers how many crafts of each recipe it already made (counted from the products arriving in your bags) and shrinks the Artisan list to the crafts still missing before it starts. Once everything is made, the list gets its original quantities back. The report always covers the whole list, including crafts from earlier runs. If you edit the list in between, the step starts over with the list as it is.

## Monster drops

`farm_monster_drops` gets materials that only monsters drop: it switches to your combat gearset with the highest item level, travels to where the monster lives (from Teamcraft's drop data), targets the nearest living one, walks into range and lets RotationSolver fight in its auto mode, until your bags hold the quantity you asked for. Between kills it waits at the spawn area for respawns. It stops when you die, when your HP is below 25% out of combat, at the timeout, or after a while without a kill or a drop, and switches RotationSolver off again. It needs RotationSolver and vnavmesh.
## Good to know

Stopping a crafting list (`crafting_control` with `stop`, or pausing or cancelling a job while it crafts) ends the list like Artisan's **Cancel** button. Artisan's own stop request would only pause it and keep Artisan busy.

Neither plugin offers a way to change its lists from outside. So editing a list briefly unloads the plugin, updates its file (with a backup) and loads it again. The Raphael preparation uses Artisan's internals and may need an update after Artisan changes.
