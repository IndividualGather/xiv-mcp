Planning works on its own. Crafting and gathering go through **Artisan** and **GatherBuddy Reborn**, so their tools are only offered while those plugins are loaded, with their own modules under **Integrations maintained by XIV MCP**.

| Tool | What it does | Module |
|---|---|---|
| `plan_craft` | Plans a project: recipe tree, crafting order, stock from bags and retainers, where to get missing materials, bag space and batches, and the stats Artisan will craft with | Game data |
| `get_crafting_lists` / `set_crafting_list` / `delete_crafting_list` | Artisan's lists and state; creates, edits or deletes lists | Artisan |
| `craft_item` / `crafting_control` | Crafts an item a number of times; starts, pauses, resumes or stops a list | Artisan |
| `prepare_craft_plan` | Creates the Artisan list in the right order and works out the **Raphael** solution for each recipe ahead of time, with the exact stats Artisan will use | Artisan |
| `run_crafting_list` | Runs an Artisan list to the end and reports what was crafted | Artisan |
| `get_gather_lists` / `set_gather_list` / `delete_gather_list` / `set_auto_gather` | GatherBuddy Reborn's auto-gather lists, and auto-gather on or off | GatherBuddy Reborn |
| `gather_until` | Gathers until your bags hold the target quantities. Your other lists are paused and restored afterwards. | GatherBuddy Reborn |

## Filling the bags

With `"quantity": "fill"`, `plan_craft` plans as many as fit in your bags. For collectables, it also plans no more than can be turned in before the scrip cap, with the scrips per collectability tier.

## A whole project as one job

> "Craft as many of this collectable as I can turn in before the scrip cap, gather what is missing, and turn them in."

Your assistant can run this as one [background job](/docs/user/features/jobs):

1. `prepare_craft_plan` with `"quantity": "fill"` for a collectable,
2. `gather_until` or `transfer_retainer_items` for the materials,
3. `run_crafting_list`,
4. `turn_in_collectables` (stops before the scrip cap),
5. `buy_item` with a standing approval, and
6. `sell_item`.

## Good to know

Neither plugin offers a way to change its lists from outside. So editing a list briefly unloads the plugin, updates its file (with a backup) and loads it again. The Raphael preparation uses Artisan's internals and may need an update after Artisan changes.
