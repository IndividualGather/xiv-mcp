These tools have their own module, **Gold Saucer**, under **Integrations maintained by XIV MCP**: reading is allowed by default, everything that plays or changes something is denied until you allow it.

- The **Fashion Report** needs no plugin.
- **Triple Triad** reading (cards, opponents, decks) and saving a deck work with **Saucy** or **TriadBuddy**.
- **Playing** Triple Triad and the **Cactpot** lotteries need **Saucy**.

## Fashion Report

80 points are enough for the full MGP reward; 100 also gives the title "Fashion Leader", once. [fashionreportxiv.com](https://fashionreportxiv.com) lists, for each of the week's hints, the items known to match it, and the week's dye colors. XIV MCP uses those lists and what you have to put an outfit together that aims for 80 points, or 100 if you ask for it.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `get_fashion_report` | This week's theme and hints, and the outfit XIV MCP would put together for you, with the expected score and where each piece comes from. Changes nothing. Also when judging opens and closes, and whether you unlocked the Fashion Report. Until the site posts this week's set, it only says which week the site shows and which week it is now. Goes online (*Online lookups*). |
| `complete_fashion_report` | Puts that outfit together in one background job, aiming for 80 points (or 100): gets the pieces, dyes them, puts them on and presents the outfit. |
| `present_fashion_report` | Puts on the given pieces, walks to the Masked Rose and presents your outfit for judging. |

> "Do this week's Fashion Report for 80 points."

### How the job works

`complete_fashion_report` plans the cheapest outfit that reaches the score, then starts one job for it:

1. **Choosing the pieces.** For each hint, any item the site lists for it counts, not only the site's own pick. For the other slots, anything you wear or carry does. XIV MCP prefers what you already wear, then what you carry, then what a retainer or your armoire holds, then buying from a vendor (only one that sells it to you: seasonal event shops only sell an event's items to players who did its quests), then the market board (at most `max_market_price` gil, 50,000 by default). The glamour dresser is only used for the site's own piece of a slot. With **AutoDuty**, a dungeon that drops one of the site's pieces is a last way. Only pieces your current job and level can wear are chosen, and a piece that also covers another slot (a robe without legs) is never used. A slot with nothing in it gets a piece, since it scores nothing.
2. **The dyes**, with `dye_item`. Only the week's exact color counts, on the weapon and the five armour slots, and only where the score needs it. Until the site has found the week's dyes, the plan leaves them out. A piece that already has the color in either of its two dye channels is used as it is, also one a retainer holds. A piece without dye, or one the job bought, is dyed; so is the empty second channel of a piece that has two. **A dye you put on a piece is only replaced after you approve in game**: the approval lists each piece with its old and new color. XIV MCP only asks when no outfit reaches the score without it; if you decline, nothing is done. Most colors come from **Standard Spectrum Dye** now. Each color uses one dye, so the job buys one for every color your bags do not cover.
3. **Presenting** as the last step: it puts on the pieces (a ring for the left hand on the left hand), walks to the Masked Rose and presents. The best of your 4 weekly attempts counts.

When no outfit reaches the score, nothing starts, and your assistant is told how far the best one gets. The plan also says about how much gil it spends, so you can settle for 80 when 100 would cost a lot. If you already have the Fashion Leader title, your assistant is told that 100 adds nothing over 80.

Vendors that only stand inside houses (such as a Journeyman Salvager hired into a home) are never used: XIV MCP does not know which houses you can enter. Neither are seasonal event shops whose items you have not unlocked by their event quests.

<Callout title="How the score is counted">
  The game does not publish the scoring. XIV MCP uses the rules players have worked out: the weapon and each armour slot are worth 10 points, each accessory 8. A slot with a hint scores in full only with a matching piece, and 2 points otherwise. An empty slot scores 0. The week's exact dye on the weapon or an armour piece adds 2. In game, a dye of the right color family adds 1, but XIV MCP does not count it, because the game's own groups of colors do not match the Fashion Report's families. If those rules are right, the expected score can be a little low, never too high.
</Callout>

Right before presenting, XIV MCP uses a **Gold Saucer VIP Card** from your bags (more MGP), unless its bonus is already active. The card only works while standing still, so XIV MCP stops, gets off the mount and waits a moment first. Turn this off in `/xivmcp` → Modules → Gold Saucer → *Use a Gold Saucer VIP Card for the Fashion Report* (on by default), or pass `use_vip_card` to `present_fashion_report`. Without a card in your bags, the job presents anyway.

After the judging, the job puts your gearset back on and, once you approve in game (the approval lists the pieces), **throws away the pieces it bought** (from a vendor or the market board) that are worth 5,000 gil or less: the vendor price, otherwise the lowest market board listing on your world. Only pieces the job itself bought can be thrown away: pieces you had already, or took from a retainer, the glamour dresser or the armoire, never are, and neither is a piece whose value is unknown. Turn this off or change the limit in `/xivmcp` → Modules → Gold Saucer → *Discard cheap pieces bought for the Fashion Report*. The gearset's glamour plate can't be applied in the Gold Saucer, so put it on again somewhere else.

The Masked Rose judges from Friday 08:00 UTC until the weekly reset on Tuesday. Outside that, the job only prepares the outfit.

### When the set is not ready

fashionreportxiv.com posts each week some time after judging opens on Friday: first the hints, then the dyes once players have found them, then the ready-made sets. From the Tuesday reset until then, it still shows the week before. XIV MCP counts the weeks since the first Fashion Report in January 2018, the way the weekly guides number them, so it knows which week it is; a set counts as this week's when the site shows this week and has posted the set.

Before `complete_fashion_report` starts a job while judging is closed, or while the site's hints are not this week's, XIV MCP asks you in game. The approval says why: when judging opens, and which week the hints are for, with the planned outfit (each piece, where it comes from, and what is dyed). If you decline, nothing is done, and your assistant is told what the approval said. The plan only needs the hints, so it can start before the site posts its ready-made sets.

The site's guide is kept for 5 minutes, so `get_fashion_report` and `complete_fashion_report` called one after another download it once.

## Triple Triad

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_triad_npcs` | Opponents: where they stand, their rules, the match fee, the cards they give (which you have, which wait in your bags) and whether you have beaten them |
| `list_triad_cards` | Cards with stars, type and the numbers on each side, whether you have them, and who gives them |
| `get_triad_decks` | Your five decks |
| `set_triad_deck` | Saves five of your cards as one of your decks |
| `build_triad_deck` | Builds the best deck against an opponent and saves it as deck 5 |
| `play_triple_triad` | Goes to an opponent and plays a number of matches, or until a card drops |
| `farm_triad_cards` | Collects missing cards: from one opponent, or from opponent to opponent |
| `get_saucy_stats` | How many matches were played, won and lost with Saucy, and the MGP and cards won |
| `stop_saucy` | Stops Saucy's Triple Triad automation |

Things to ask:

> "Collect all the Triple Triad cards I can still win from NPCs."

> "Which Triple Triad cards am I missing that Gold Saucer NPCs give?"

> "Get me the Lightning card from Triple Triad Trader."

> "Build the best deck against Joellaut and play five matches with it."

### How playing works

1. XIV MCP goes to the opponent: it teleports and walks with Lifestream and vnavmesh, or shows you the way and waits.
2. It talks to them to start the match.
3. Saucy picks the deck, plays and rematches until it has played enough matches, until a card drops, or (for all of an opponent's cards) until every card you are missing from them has dropped, with its "Farm all NPC cards once" mode and "Missing cards only".
4. For a card or all cards, XIV MCP checks when Saucy stops whether you have what you wanted, and starts another round if not.

A card that drops arrives in your bags as an item. It counts as had for "until you have the card", but only joins your collection once you use (register) it.

`farm_triad_cards` runs as a [background job](/docs/user/features/jobs), so it can keep going for hours. Follow it in `/xivmcp` → **Jobs** or on the [overlay](/docs/user/overlay), and cancel it there.

### Collecting every card you can win

`list_triad_cards` with `source: "npc"` lists only the cards opponents give, with a summary: how many you are missing, from how many opponents, and in which zones.

`farm_triad_cards` without an opponent starts one job for all of them:

- **The route** starts in your current zone, then goes zone by zone, always to the nearest opponent next. `zone` limits it to one area, `max_npcs` (20 by default) keeps the job to a manageable size.
- **Each step** plays one opponent until you have all of their missing cards. A card that several opponents give is only farmed once, and an opponent left with nothing missing is skipped.
- **Unavailable opponents** are left out and listed: those that need an unlock quest first, and (with `skip_unavailable`, on by default) those that only play at certain Eorzea hours and are outside them right now.
- **During the job** you can skip an opponent (`update_job` with skip) or cancel it. If an opponent turns you away, the step fails and the job waits for you or your assistant to skip it.

`list_triad_npcs` shows for each opponent whether they can be challenged: `unlocked`, the `unlockQuests` still needed, their `hours`, and `availableNow`.

### Decks

- `deck` on `play_triple_triad` and `farm_triad_cards` picks deck 1-5, `optimized` (Saucy builds the best deck into deck 5 first), or `recommended` (the game's own pick). Without it, Saucy's own setting is used.
- `set_triad_deck` checks the game's rules first: 5 different cards you have, at most one 5-star card and at most two cards of 4 stars or more.
- `build_triad_deck` uses Saucy's deck optimizer. It simulates matches with your cards against the opponent's rules for a few minutes and keeps the best deck.

## Cactpot

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `play_mini_cactpot` | Goes to the Mini Cactpot Broker and plays today's tickets, up to 3 (10 MGP each). Saucy picks the spots. |
| `play_jumbo_cactpot` | Goes to the Jumbo Cactpot Cashier. Saucy collects last week's prizes, walks to the broker and buys this week's tickets with your 4-digit numbers, or random ones (3 a week, for 100, 150 and 200 MGP). |

Saucy walks from the cashier to the broker with vnavmesh. Without it, Saucy stops after the cashier, and you talk to the broker yourself.

Right before talking to the cashier, who pays out last week's prizes, XIV MCP uses a **Gold Saucer VIP Card** from your bags (more MGP), unless its bonus is already active. The card only works while standing still, so XIV MCP stops, gets off the mount and waits a moment first. Turn this off in `/xivmcp` → Modules → Gold Saucer → *Use a Gold Saucer VIP Card for the Jumbo Cactpot* (on by default), or pass `use_vip_card` to `play_jumbo_cactpot`. Without a card in your bags, it plays anyway.

## What it changes in Saucy

Saucy has no interface for other plugins. XIV MCP sends it `/saucy` commands, and changes its settings in memory for as long as a tool runs (which deck, the deck optimizer, the Cactpot modules and ticket numbers). Afterwards it puts them back. Saucy's settings file is not touched.

GATEs, Cuff-a-Cur and Out on a Limb are not covered: Saucy cannot be told to start them from outside.
