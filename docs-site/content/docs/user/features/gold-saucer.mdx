These tools work through **Saucy**, so they are only offered while Saucy is loaded. Reading your cards, the Triple Triad opponents and your decks, and saving a deck, also work with **TriadBuddy** instead. They have their own module, **Gold Saucer**, under **Integrations maintained by XIV MCP**: reading is allowed by default, everything that plays or changes something is denied until you allow it.

## Triple Triad

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `list_triad_npcs` | Opponents: where they stand, their rules, the match fee, the cards they give (which you have, which wait in your bags) and whether you have beaten them |
| `list_triad_cards` | Cards with stars, type and the numbers on each side, whether you have them, and who gives them |
| `get_triad_decks` | Your five decks |
| `set_triad_deck` | Saves five of your cards as one of your decks |
| `build_triad_deck` | Builds the best deck against an opponent and saves it as deck 5 |
| `play_triple_triad` | Goes to an opponent and plays a number of matches, or until a card drops |
| `farm_triad_cards` | Collects missing cards: from one opponent, or from opponent to opponent |
| `get_saucy_stats` | How many matches were played, won and lost with Saucy, and the MGP and cards won |
| `stop_saucy` | Stops Saucy's Triple Triad automation |

Things to ask:

> "Collect all the Triple Triad cards I can still win from NPCs."

> "Which Triple Triad cards am I missing that Gold Saucer NPCs give?"

> "Get me the Lightning card from Triple Triad Trader."

> "Build the best deck against Joellaut and play five matches with it."

### How playing works

1. XIV MCP goes to the opponent: it teleports and walks with Lifestream and vnavmesh, or shows you the way and waits.
2. It talks to them to start the match.
3. Saucy picks the deck, plays and rematches until it has played enough matches, or until a card drops.
4. For a card or all cards, XIV MCP checks after each drop whether you have what you wanted, and starts another round if not.

A card that drops arrives in your bags as an item. It counts as had for "until you have the card", but only joins your collection once you use (register) it.

`farm_triad_cards` runs as a [background job](/docs/user/features/jobs), so it can keep going for hours. Follow it in `/xivmcp` → **Jobs** or on the [overlay](/docs/user/overlay), and cancel it there.

### Collecting every card you can win

`list_triad_cards` with `source: "npc"` lists only the cards opponents give, with a summary: how many you are missing, from how many opponents, and in which zones.

`farm_triad_cards` without an opponent starts one job for all of them:

- **The route** starts in your current zone, then goes zone by zone, always to the nearest opponent next. `zone` limits it to one area, `max_npcs` (20 by default) keeps the job to a manageable size.
- **Each step** plays one opponent until you have all of their missing cards. A card that several opponents give is only farmed once, and an opponent left with nothing missing is skipped.
- **Unavailable opponents** are left out and listed: those that need an unlock quest first, and (with `skip_unavailable`, on by default) those that only play at certain Eorzea hours and are outside them right now.
- **During the job** you can skip an opponent (`update_job` with skip) or cancel it. If an opponent turns you away, the step fails and the job waits for you or your assistant to skip it.

`list_triad_npcs` shows for each opponent whether they can be challenged: `unlocked`, the `unlockQuests` still needed, their `hours`, and `availableNow`.

### Decks

- `deck` on `play_triple_triad` and `farm_triad_cards` picks deck 1-5, `optimized` (Saucy builds the best deck into deck 5 first), or `recommended` (the game's own pick). Without it, Saucy's own setting is used.
- `set_triad_deck` checks the game's rules first: 5 different cards you have, at most one 5-star card and at most two cards of 4 stars or more.
- `build_triad_deck` uses Saucy's deck optimizer. It simulates matches with your cards against the opponent's rules for a few minutes and keeps the best deck.

## Cactpot

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `play_mini_cactpot` | Goes to the Mini Cactpot Broker and plays today's tickets, up to 3 (10 MGP each). Saucy picks the spots. |
| `play_jumbo_cactpot` | Goes to the Jumbo Cactpot Cashier. Saucy collects last week's prizes, walks to the broker and buys this week's tickets with your 4-digit numbers, or random ones (100 MGP each, 3 a week). |

Saucy walks from the cashier to the broker with vnavmesh. Without it, Saucy stops after the cashier, and you talk to the broker yourself.

## What it changes in Saucy

Saucy has no interface for other plugins. XIV MCP sends it `/saucy` commands, and changes its settings in memory for as long as a tool runs (which deck, the deck optimizer, the Cactpot modules and ticket numbers). Afterwards it puts them back. Saucy's settings file is not touched.

GATEs, Cuff-a-Cur and Out on a Limb are not covered: Saucy cannot be told to start them from outside.
