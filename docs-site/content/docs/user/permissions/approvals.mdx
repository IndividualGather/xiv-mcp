Some actions are never decided by your assistant alone:

- buying with anything other than gil,
- recalling a running venture,
- completing a trade with another player, with what both sides hold,
- and anything set to *Ask*, whether a module of XIV MCP or a capability of a third-party plugin.

They open an approval window in the game, and nothing happens until you answer.

- **Who asks:** your assistant, or a named plugin.
- **What:** the tool, what it wants to do and its arguments. The window is highlighted by risk.
- **Your answer:** approve once, approve for this session (that tool is approved until the game or XIV MCP restarts), always allow (which sets the setting to *Allow*), or decline. For a third-party action that cannot be undone, the window asks every time and only offers *once*.
- **No answer** within two minutes counts as declined.
- **You notice it** even in another window: the game's taskbar button flashes while an approval waits.

A plugin can also ask in the middle of a call, with the real numbers, such as "Buy 3 Hi-Potions for 1,200 gil".

One `buy_item` call asks once, for the whole quantity, even when it buys in several batches of 99. Buying the same item again in a new call asks again.

## Standing approvals

For jobs that buy repeatedly, a **standing approval** asks once: "spend up to this much of this currency (on these items, for this long)". Your assistant asks for it with `request_spending_approval`, and later purchases with `buy_item` use it without asking again.

- The question stays up for **five minutes**. Your assistant doesn't wait that long: it gets an answer within 20 seconds, either yours or "still waiting", and checks `list_approvals` later. Until you approve, nothing can be bought with it; a declined or unanswered one never becomes usable.
- Its time ("for this long") starts when you approve it.
- Every purchase under it counts its real cost, measured from your currency balance.
- It stops when the approved amount is used up.
- It stops if a purchase turns out to be paid with another currency.

Active approvals are listed in `/xivmcp` → **Jobs**, with what has been spent and what is left, and a **Revoke** button. Your assistant can also list them (`list_approvals`) and revoke them (`revoke_approval`).
