XIV MCP itself sends nothing anywhere: it has no account, no telemetry and no server of its own. Data leaves your PC in two ways, and you control both.

## What your AI app sees

Every tool result goes to your AI app, and from there to its AI model, together with your messages. Unless you use a model on your own PC (see [LM Studio](/docs/user/connect/lm-studio)), the model runs in the cloud of the company behind the app, under that company's privacy terms.

What is in those results depends on what you allow:

| Module | What your assistant can see |
|---|---|
| **Game data** | Your character's name, world, jobs, gear, inventory, currencies, quests and collections, and the names of players in your party and around you |
| **Items & retainers** | Your retainers, their inventories and ventures, the FC chest |
| **Market & purchases** | Your listings and sale history |
| **Game & navigation** | Your other characters, their worlds and service accounts, and game windows and menus on screen |
| **Plugin management** | Other plugins' settings, which can contain passwords or keys. Denied by default. |
| **Screen** | Screenshots, which can show chat, tells and other players' names. Every screenshot asks you first. |

To keep something from your assistant, set its module's *Reading* to *Deny*, or switch the module off. See [Modules and permissions](/docs/user/permissions/modules).

## What goes online

Only the **Online lookups** module contacts websites, and it is denied by default:

| Website | What it is asked |
|---|---|
| **universalis.app** | Market prices for the items and the world or data center your assistant asks about |
| **FFXIV Teamcraft** (raw.githubusercontent.com) | Item source data, downloaded once and kept in `pluginConfigs/XivMcp/teamcraft/` |
| **ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com** | An item's wiki page, when your assistant asks for it |

None of these requests contain your character's name or your access token.

## What stays on your PC

The [caches](/docs/user/caches), the audit log, your settings and the access token are kept in Dalamud's `pluginConfigs` folder (`XivMcp.json` and the `XivMcp` folder next to it) and are never sent anywhere by XIV MCP. When you report a bug, leave out your access token, and check logs and screenshots for names before you post them.
